# Hero artwork generation record

Mode: built-in image generation tool.

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept
>
> Asset type: wide website hero artwork for a PhD research portfolio
>
> Primary request: Create a visually arresting editorial scientific artwork representing an interdisciplinary cell biologist studying cytoskeletal dynamics, molecular signalling, mitochondria, and microbial systems. This is decorative concept art, not a scientific data figure.
>
> Scene/backdrop: deep near-black blue-green field with subtle microscope grain and generous quiet negative space on the left for website typography.
>
> Subject: on the right two-thirds, a luminous living-cell landscape where branching actin filaments sweep like a directional current, a translucent cell membrane folds through space, a mitochondrion-like structure glows within, and small molecular scaffold nodes connect along the filaments. Suggest movement, signalling, structure, and survival without literal labels.
>
> Style/medium: premium scientific editorial illustration, hybrid of confocal microscopy, macro biological photography, etched research-plate linework, and refined 3D rendering; sophisticated and credible, not sci-fi fantasy.
>
> Composition/framing: panoramic 16:9 composition, strong asymmetric focal mass on the right, dark readable area on the left, layered depth, subtle diagonal flow from lower right toward upper center.
>
> Lighting/mood: bioluminescent, precise, exploratory, calm confidence.
>
> Color palette: deep ink navy and dark teal, electric cyan, mineral mint, restrained coral-orange highlights, small warm ivory details.
>
> Materials/textures: translucent membranes, fibrous actin, wet cellular surfaces, microscopic particles, faint technical contour lines.
>
> Constraints: no people, no lab equipment, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no fake charts, no UI mockup, no anatomical organs. Keep it elegant and suitable for a serious PhD application.
>
> Avoid: purple gradient aesthetic, generic blobs, neon cyberpunk, medical stock imagery, cluttered full-frame composition.

## Research-world panorama

Mode: built-in image generation tool.

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept
>
> Asset type: panoramic chapter-transition artwork for an interactive PhD research portfolio
>
> Primary request: Create one continuous scientific editorial panorama that transitions through three connected biological worlds: molecular signalling inside a mammalian cell, conserved membrane-protein structure inside a lipid bilayer, and coral-associated microbial survival. The visual should feel like travelling deeper through biological scale, not three separate poster panels.
>
> Scene/backdrop: deep ink navy biological space with uninterrupted flow across the canvas.
>
> Subject: left zone shows paired fluorescent protein fragments approaching along actin filaments with one precise green complementation glow; central zone shows an elegant multi-pass membrane protein embedded in a translucent lipid bilayer with structural ribbons and conserved residue points; right zone transforms the molecular field into coral microtexture with bacterial colonies and restrained antimicrobial inhibition halos.
>
> Style/medium: premium scientific editorial concept art combining confocal microscopy, refined 3D rendering, macro biological photography and etched research-atlas linework; credible and sophisticated, decorative concept art rather than experimental data.
>
> Composition/framing: ultra-wide horizontal composition, cinematic depth, continuous left-to-right flow, clear visual breathing room, no hard dividers.
>
> Lighting/mood: bioluminescent, precise, investigative, calm confidence.
>
> Color palette: near-black navy, deep teal, electric cyan, mineral mint, restrained coral-orange and warm ivory.
>
> Materials/textures: fibrous actin, translucent lipid membrane, ribbon protein, wet coral microtexture, microscopic particles.
>
> Constraints: no people, no laboratory equipment, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no charts, no UI mockup. Scientifically inspired but do not present it as measured data.
>
> Avoid: generic purple gradients, fantasy space imagery, cyberpunk neon, stock medical imagery, clutter.
